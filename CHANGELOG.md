# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — unreleased

Breaking: shared code moves out of the Mermaid and Graphviz namespaces; the README's "Upgrading from 0.1" table maps each old class to its new one.

- PlantUML activity diagrams draw swimlanes natively (`Activities\Lane`, `Ftile\SwimlaneDrawing`, `Ftile\FtileLane`), as PlantUML's swimlanes draw them: the steps laid out once, each swimlane as wide as what it holds, `skinparam swimlaneWidth` or its title, set between dividers, its title centred over it and its colour behind it, each step moved across with its swimlane and the arrows from one swimlane to the next bent across. Decisions draw their branches in other swimlanes, their arrows bent across as PlantUML bends them — round the test's left when its swimlane is left of its branch's — and, as PlantUML, a stop beside a decision in neither its swimlane nor its branch's is not drawn. Loops draw their steps, tests and backward actions in other swimlanes too, their arrows back bent across as PlantUML's; a stop after a loop in another swimlane is, as PlantUML, drawn only in a swimlane the loop holds. Forks, splits and merges draw their flows in other swimlanes, their bars and lines in the swimlanes PlantUML puts them in — a fork's below where its last `fork again` or its end was written, a split's with its first and last flows — and their arrows bent across. Switches draw their cases in other swimlanes, arrows into and out of them bent across into their diamonds' sides as PlantUML's; as PlantUML, a case of a single step under a wide test is drawn again in each swimlane the switch stands in. Else-ifs draw their branches in other swimlanes too, the line under them drawn in each swimlane between the first and the last a branch leaves in, as PlantUML draws it. A group holding steps of another swimlane is still left to PlantUML, as is what stands outside any swimlane. They went to PlantUML before.
- PlantUML activity diagrams draw else-ifs down under `!pragma useVerticalIf on` natively (`Ftile\FtileIfLongVertical`), each test under the one before with its branch on its right, as PlantUML lays them out. They went to PlantUML before.
- PlantUML activity diagrams draw `skinparam conditionEndStyle hline` natively, each decision closed on a line across under its branches rather than a diamond, as PlantUML closes it (`Ftile\ConditionEnd`). It went to PlantUML before.
- PlantUML activity diagrams draw arrows of several colours (`-[#red;#blue]->`) natively, each colour beside the one before as PlantUML's worm mutation moves it (`Ftile\WormMutation`, `Rainbow::$stripes`), `skinparam colorArrowSeparationSpace` apart. They went to PlantUML before. `tools/record-plantuml-parity.php` takes the fixtures to record as a pattern.
- PlantUML activity diagrams draw `switch`, `case` and `endswitch` natively (`Activities\Instruction\InstructionSwitch`, `Ftile\FtileSwitch`, `Ftile\SwitchArrows`), as PlantUML's switch tiles lay them out: the cases side by side under the test's diamond, spread under it when it is wider, the arrows in labelled with each case and the arrows out meeting at a diamond below, its own notes at its top. They went to PlantUML before. The shared activity model gains `Model\Activity\Choice`, a switch, which PlantUML's writer writes back as one and the flowchart lowering draws as one test.
- PlantUML class, state and description diagrams are set as svek sets them: what is drawn moved 6 in from the top left as PlantUML's limit finder measures it, 15 more room after it, and no document margin; a composite state's inner image is laid out with dot's own separations, as svek writes it; an edge label keeps 1 point of room each side (6 on a loop); `X[H]` stands inside `X`; a folder keeps room for its hidden title; a container's title stands as low as PlantUML sets it for its symbol; a database's outline no longer crosses itself.
- PlantUML text is set a line apart as PlantUML measures its default font (Java's SansSerif, 1.164 times its size), not by the bundled font's line; svek wraps each cluster in the two unlabelled clusters it protects them with, gives a cluster's label the size PlantUML's cluster header measures, and sizes each region of a composite state as an image of its own; a package's or folder's title stands 4 in and 2 down from its corner; an interface is a circle 16 across, its name hanging below its node; an actor's, a boundary's, a control's or an entity's name stands right under its icon; a link's end labels stand as dot sets a tail and a head label, a corner on the line's end on its left, the head's moved across when it would meet the tail's.
- The native dot engine, laid out left to right or right to left, orders each rank from its other end and mirrors the result, as `dot` does, but for a cluster holding no other cluster, whose nodes therefore lie the other way round; on 300 random graphs its orders agree with `dot`'s 54% of the time without clusters and 38% with them, from 39% and 25%.
- The native dot engine keeps each flat edge that constrains ranks pointing forward along its rank, breaking cycles of them as `dot` does, and meets edges node by node, each node's out-edges in turn, as `dot` builds its ordering; on 300 random graphs its orders agree with `dot`'s 61% of the time without clusters and 44% with them, from 54% and 38%.
- The native dot engine pulls a long edge's real ends level no harder than a plain edge's, as `dot` does, where it pulled them twice as hard; on the 300 random graphs, nodes now stand 17% nearer where `dot` puts them. Its graph's bounds lie at what it holds, with a margin around clusters, where network simplex could leave them further out; the drawing's size now matches `dot`'s more often.
- The native DOT renderer draws `record` and `Mrecord` nodes, which went to the `dot` command before: fields, `{}` groups turned with `rankdir`, field ports, and compass points on any node. A malformed record label shows the node's name, as `dot` does.
- The native DOT renderer draws images, a node's `image` (with `imagescale` and `imagepos`) and an HTML cell's `<IMG/>`, read only from the directories a host allows: `Viz`'s new `imageDirectories` argument, `DotPipeline`'s (`GraphViz\DotImages`, `DotImage`, `Drawing\DotPictures`, `Html\HtmlImage`). A name must resolve inside one, to a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP file; any other image leaves the graph to `dot`, as every image did before.
- `ratio=compress` no longer sends a graph to the `dot` command: it leaves `dot`'s layouts as they are, and the native ones too.
- `layout=nop` (`nop1`, `nop2`) is drawn natively (`Layout\Nop\NopEngine`, `GraphViz\DotPositions`): a graph laid out already draws where its `pos`, edge splines and `lp` put it. Every Graphviz layout is now native.
- `layout=sfdp`, `osage` and `patchwork` are drawn natively (`Layout\Osage\OsageEngine`, `Layout\Patchwork\PatchworkEngine`; sfdp as fdp without clusters), which went to the `dot` command before.
- `layout=fdp` is drawn natively, with its clusters (`Layout\Fdp\FdpEngine`, level by level by the stress majorization neato uses, now `Layout\Free\Stress`), with `K`, which went to the `dot` command before.
- `layout=circo` is drawn natively (`Layout\Circo\CircoEngine`, `CircoBlocks`), block by block on circles after Six and Tollis, with `mindist`, which went to the `dot` command before.
- `layout=twopi` is drawn natively (`Layout\Twopi\TwopiEngine`, radially as Wills describes it, with `ranksep` and `root`), which went to the `dot` command before. neato and twopi share `Layout\Free\FreePlacement`.
- The native DOT renderer draws nodes named by numbers, such as `digraph { 1 -> 2 }`, which failed with a type error.
- `layout=neato` is drawn natively (`Layout\Neato\NeatoEngine`, by stress majorization from classical scaling, as Gansner, Koren and North describe it), which went to the `dot` command before. `LayoutEdge::$length` carries an edge's `len`.
- The native DOT renderer draws `splines=ortho` (`GraphViz\Drawing\DotOrtho`), which went to the `dot` command before: square edges along the layout's virtual nodes, each on its own track across the gaps between ranks and spread across the sides of its nodes.
- The native DOT renderer draws HTML in `xlabel`, `headlabel` and `taillabel`, which went to the `dot` command before; `DotNodes::endText()` and `externalText()` return a `DotLabel`.
- The native DOT renderer draws radial gradients at an angle, their focus moved as `dot` moves it (`Draw\GradientAxis::focus()`, `radial()`), and colour lists on node and cluster outlines, in their first colour, which went to the `dot` command before.
- The native DOT renderer draws gradients at any `gradientangle` and weighted gradient lists, split sharply as `dot` splits them, which went to the `dot` command before (`Draw\GradientAxis`, `Mark::$gradientAngle`, `Mark::$gradientSplit`), in SVG, PNG and PDF. `DotColors::fill()` takes no angle; a turned drawing turns its gradients.
- The native DOT renderer draws `concentrate=true`, which went to the `dot` command before: edges leaving or reaching a node the same way merge into trunks (`Layout\Dot\Concentration`, `LayoutGraph::$concentrate`), each stretch drawn once in parts that join where they branch (`Placement::$pieces`). It merges more than `dot`, which merges only chains its ordering leaves side by side.
- The native dot engine orders large graphs two to three times faster, its output unchanged: transposition passes over ranks whose neighbourhood did not change, swaps two lone nodes in place, and costs both orders of a pair in one pass over their edges (`DotCrossings::both()`, which replaces `pair()`).
- The native dot engine honours `ratio` as a number or `fill` (`LayoutGraph::$aspect`), stretching positions as `dot` does. `pos` and `pin`, which `dot` ignores, no longer send a graph to the `dot` command.
- The native DOT renderer draws the `radial` style, on nodes, clusters and HTML tables, which went to the `dot` command before: drawings carry radial gradients (`Draw\Shading`), which the SVG, PNG and PDF backends paint. PNG gradients between colours without alpha were painted transparent; `Color\ColorValue::mixed()` now takes such colours as opaque.
- The native DOT renderer turns drawings to landscape (`Draw\Units::turned()`) for `rotate=90`, `landscape` and an `orientation` starting with `l`, which went to the `dot` command before; other `rotate` values, which `dot` ignores, no longer leave the native renderer.
- The native DOT renderer draws the `striped` and `wedged` styles (`GraphViz\Drawing\DotFills`), which went to the `dot` command before: a box's or a cluster's colours side by side, an ellipse's in slices, weighted as `dot` weighs them. Only `radial` still goes to `dot`.
- The native DOT renderer draws an edge's colour list (`GraphViz\Drawing\DotStrokes`), which went to the `dot` command before: side by side, or one after the other where weighted, with arrowheads in the colours `dot` gives them. Edge geometry moved into `GraphViz\Drawing\DotEdgePaths`.
- The native DOT renderer draws `samehead` and `sametail`, which went to the `dot` command before: edges sharing one meet at a single point on their node's outline.
- The native DOT renderer honours `splines`: `line` and `false` draw straight edges, `polyline` the corners of each edge's shortest path (`Placement::$polylines`), and `none` or an empty value no edges, where every value but `ortho` drew splines.
- The native dot engine counts ports, as `dot` does (`LayoutEdge::$tailPort`, `$headPort`, measured by `GraphViz\Drawing\DotPorts`): crossing minimisation orders nodes by where across their neighbours the edges' ports stand, and coordinates pull ports level, so a record's fields lead to the nodes below them in order.
- The native dot engine routes edges as splines (`Layout\Dot\DotSplines`, `Placement::$splines`), as Dobkin et al. describe: through a corridor of boxes clear of the nodes and clusters an edge passes, round a cluster's corner where it reaches between ranks, by the shortest path, fitted with cubic Béziers. Edges between the same two nodes spread apart as `dot` spreads them, where they were drawn one over the other. The native DOT drawing draws them, rather than a curve smoothed through the virtual nodes, and bends them to the edges' ports.
- The native DOT renderer honours `decorate`: an edge's label is underlined and joined to the edge's middle, as `dot` draws it.
- The native DOT renderer honours `pad`: the drawing is padded by it, one length for both sides or two, as `dot` pads it, rather than always by 4 points.
- The native DOT renderer fills with gradients: a `fillcolor` or `bgcolor` list on a node, a cluster, the graph or an HTML table or cell is drawn from its first colour to its second, across, or back across when `gradientangle` points left, where it went to the `dot` command before. Weighted lists, other angles and `color` lists still go to `dot`.
- The native DOT renderer draws HTML labels on nodes, edges, clusters and the graph, which went to the `dot` command before: styled text (`<FONT>`, `<B>`, `<I>`, `<U>`, `<O>`, `<S>`, `<SUB>`, `<SUP>`, `<BR/>`), and tables with spans, borders and the sides they cover (`SIDES`), rounded, dashed, dotted or invisible tables and cells (`STYLE`), spacing, padding, colours, sizes, alignment, rules cell by cell as `dot` draws them (`<HR/>`, `<VR/>`, `ROWS="*"`, `COLUMNS="*"`), nesting and cell ports. `<IMG>`, HTML outside `label`, and malformed labels still go to `dot`.
- The native DOT renderer draws `headlabel`, `taillabel` and `xlabel`, which it ignored before: end labels in their `labelfont*` font, by `labelangle` and `labeldistance` where set and beside the end as `dot` places them otherwise; external labels off a corner of their node or edge's middle, clear of other labels, the drawing grown to hold them.
- The native DOT renderer clips an edge at the cluster its `lhead` or `ltail` names in a `compound` graph, and leaves an end unclipped where `headclip` or `tailclip` is false; it ignored all four before.
- The native dot engine honours `ordering=out` and `ordering=in`, on a node or the whole graph, keeping a node's edges out, or in, in the order they come, as `dot` does; it ignored them before.
- The native dot engine holds edges between nodes of one `group` straight, weighing them a hundred times more, as `dot` does; it ignored `group` before.
- A classifier's body and a note over several lines are read whole, as PlantUML's commands for them take every line: a member such as `title : String` is no longer the diagram's title. A note on a link is Rose's note, merged with the link's label below it, above it or beside it, as svek merges them. A frame's tab is as wide as its title and 10 more, its title in it, as PlantUML draws it. A node and a database take 10 more room past a bottom corner, as PlantUML's symbols do. A link's end labels stand where dot puts them on the whole line, then move off any node they come within 8 of, as svek moves them. A cloud is drawn as PlantUML draws it, its bubbles jittered by Java's random numbers seeded with its size (`PlantUML\Draw\JavaRandom`); a use case is as tall as the ellipse PlantUML fits around its text.
- A PlantUML `legend` with no position no longer fails. A sequence diagram stands 5 clear inside its captions, and 5 more around everything, as teoz sets it; svek's diagrams keep 10 past what they draw and 5 past their captions.
- PlantUML shadows: an element whose style sets `Shadowing`, as `skin rose` does for every element, casts a darkened copy of its outline (`PlantUML\Draw\Shadow`) and takes the room PlantUML gives it; a sequence diagram's participant boxes and stick men grow by their shadow, as Rose sizes them.
- PlantUML's header, footer, title, legend and caption blocks are 1 point larger each way than their text and padding, as its bordered text blocks are. In a state diagram's scope, svek writes the composite states drawn as images of their own first, which decides how dot breaks cycles; native svek does too.
- The native dot engine starts a cluster's initial order from its nodes in the order the cluster holds them, as `dot` does, which lifts its clustered orders' agreement with `dot` from 0.25 to 0.29 on the parity graphs. Svek wraps a cluster a link starts or ends at in two more protection clusters, and puts the link's point in it directly, as PlantUML does; that point is only added for such a cluster.
- PlantUML parity: self-authored fixtures with goldens recorded from the pinned jar (`tools/plantuml.lock`, `tools/record-plantuml-parity.php`), graded by `PlantUmlParityTest` on the words drawn, the height and where each row of text falls, each with a floor that only rises; a weekly `PlantUML parity (live)` workflow checks the goldens against the jar itself.
- PlantUML activity diagrams (the new syntax) are read natively (`PlantUML\Activities`) with PlantUML's own rules for arrows, colours, labels, notes, `kill`, `detach` and `break`, into the shared activity model, and drawn natively by a port of PlantUML's tile layout (`PlantUML\Ftile`): actions in every box style, circles, decisions side by side, down one side or across for else-ifs, while and repeat loops with backward actions and breaks, forks, merges and splits, partitions, groups, rectangles and cards, notes, arrows merged as PlantUML's snakes merge, and the whole compressed across and down as PlantUML compresses it. A lone name is refused by the description factory, as PlantUML refuses it, so `start` alone is an activity diagram. `goto`, packages and the legacy syntax are left to PlantUML.
- PlantUML sequence diagrams are read natively (`PlantUML\Sequences`) with PlantUML's own rules for arrows, activations, groups, notes and numbering, into the shared sequence model, so they convert to Mermaid, and are drawn natively by a port of teoz, PlantUML's sequence layout (`PlantUML\Teoz`): its constraint solver, participants' columns, tiles, activations, groups, notes and boxes, drawn with PlantUML's Rose components. A divider now closes off the message before it for activations, as in PlantUML; a participant may be activated twice by one message, but not again once the message deactivated it; anchored messages are left to PlantUML.
- PlantUML component, deployment and use case diagrams are drawn natively (`PlantUML\Descriptions`): every symbol PlantUML draws elements with, by keyword or short form, in containers drawn with their own symbols, with links and notes; they read into the shared use case model or the shared graph. A cluster laid out left to right keeps its label on top. The PlantUML graph writer quotes an empty label as a space, which PlantUML reads.
- PlantUML state diagrams are drawn natively (`PlantUML\States`): states, pseudostates, composite states laid out on their own or as clusters as svek decides, concurrent regions, history, bars, transitions and notes, read into the shared state model too. A factory PlantUML would fail on is passed over as PlantUML passes it over (`PlantUML\PlantUmlRefusal`); the link and note models, link drawing and box clipping are shared by the families (`PlantUML\Links\PlantUmlLink`, `PlantUML\Language\PlantUmlNote`, `PlantUML\Draw\LinkPainter`, `BoxClip`).
- PlantUML class diagrams are drawn natively (`PlantUML\Classes`): read with PlantUML's own commands, laid out as its svek lays them out on the native dot engine (`PlantUML\Svek\Svek`), and drawn with its classifier boxes, member icons, link marks (`PlantUML\Links`), packages and notes in its document frame (`PlantUML\Draw`). `PlantUML\PlantUmlReader` reads them into the shared class model, so PlantUML class diagrams convert to Mermaid and DOT. The pipeline picks a diagram family by PlantUML's factory order with commands exported from its MIT jar (`resources/plantuml/commands.json`, `tools/PlantUmlCommands.java`). `Viz` draws PlantUML natively first. `Draw\Route` and `Draw\Units::scaled()` are shared by the native drawings.
- The foundation of native PlantUML (`PlantUML\PlantUmlPipeline`), ported from PlantUML's MIT distribution: its `@startuml` block, line joins, comments and `!pragma`s (`PlantUML\Language\PlantUmlSource`); the commands every diagram shares — `skinparam`, `<style>`, `skin rose`, `title`, `caption`, `header`, `footer`, `legend` and `scale` (`PlantUmlDocument`); and its cascading styles with its default and `rose` skins and its skinparam table (`PlantUML\Style\StyleSheet`, from `resources/plantuml`, imported by `tools/import-plantuml.php`). Diagram families plug in as `PlantUmlFamily`s, tried in PlantUML's order; kinds no native family reads, the preprocessor and sprites are left to PlantUML.
- DOT draws natively: `GraphViz\DotPipeline` lays a graph out with the native dot engine and draws its node shapes, arrowheads, styles, colours, clusters and labels, with no `dot` installed. `Viz` uses it first and falls back to `dot` for what it leaves, which `GraphViz\DotFeatureGate` names: other layout engines, HTML labels, records, images, orthogonal splines and the like. `Draw\Units` converts points to drawing units, and a native pipeline refuses what it does not draw with `Source\Unsupported`.
- A native dot layout engine (`Layout\Dot\DotEngine`), written from Gansner et al.'s paper without Graphviz's source: network-simplex ranking with clusters and rank sets, virtual nodes, median and transposition ordering, network-simplex coordinates. The neutral layout input gains edge weight, constraint and label size, and rank sets. [docs/graphviz-compatibility.md](docs/graphviz-compatibility.md) tracks how closely it follows dot.
- DOT is read natively, with no `dot` installed: a lexer and parser for the whole language (`GraphViz\Language`) and a resolved model (`GraphViz\Model`) that follows dot's rules for defaults, subgraphs, strict graphs and clusters, checked against dot itself on random graphs. `GraphViz\DotReader` uses it; `GraphViz\Source\DotGraphReader` still reads `dot -Tjson0`.
- A shared state model (`Model\State`): states nested in composite states and concurrent regions, pseudostates, transitions and notes. Mermaid state diagrams read into it and are written from it; PlantUML writes it; DOT draws it as a graph.
- A shared class model (`Model\Classes`): classifiers of each kind with parsed members, relations marked at both ends with their labels, packages and notes. Mermaid class diagrams read into it and are written from it; PlantUML writes it; DOT draws each classifier as a record.
- A shared entity-relationship model (`Model\Entity`): entities with typed, keyed attributes, relationships with a cardinality at each end, and groups. Mermaid ER diagrams read into it and are written from it; PlantUML writes it in information-engineering notation; DOT draws it as a graph.
- A shared sequence model (`Model\Sequence`): participants of each kind, boxes, and steps in time (messages, notes, nested fragments, activations, creation and destruction, separators). Mermaid sequence diagrams read into it and are written from it; PlantUML writes it. It has no graph form, so DOT has none.
- A shared use-case model (`Model\UseCase`): actors and use cases, system boundaries, associations with their end marks, includes, extends and generalizations, and notes. Mermaid `usecase-beta` diagrams read into it and are written from it; PlantUML writes it; DOT draws it as a graph.
- A shared activity model (`Model\Activity`): actions, decisions with else-ifs, while and repeat loops, forks and splits, partitions, notes and terminals. PlantUML writes it in its activity syntax; Mermaid and DOT draw it as a flowchart.
- The shared graph gains compartments on a node (`Node::$compartments`) the UML arrowheads `Triangle`, `Diamond` and `HollowDiamond`, and an `Actor` shape.
- `Diagram\{Graph, Node, Edge, Cluster, Shape, ArrowHead}` become `Model\Graph\*`; `Diagram\Direction` and `Diagram\LineStyle` become `Model\Direction` and `Model\LineStyle`.
- `Layout\GraphFont` becomes `Text\FontFace`; `Mermaid\MermaidFont`, `MermaidFontCoverage`, `MermaidFallbackFonts` and `MermaidWrap` become `Text\Font`, `FontCoverage`, `FallbackFonts` and `Wrap`.
- Drawing is language-neutral: `Mermaid\MermaidDrawing` and `MermaidMark` become `Draw\Drawing` and `Draw\Mark`, whose `kind` is the `Draw\MarkKind` enum; `MermaidSvg`, `MermaidPng` and `MermaidPdf` become `Draw\SvgBackend`, `PngBackend` and `PdfBackend`; the rough look moves to `Draw\Sketch` and the curves to `Layout\Curve`. `MermaidDrawing::scene()` and `::graph()` become `Mermaid\MermaidPainter`.
- `Mermaid\MermaidRenderer` gives way to `Draw\NativeRenderer`, which draws any `Draw\Pipeline`; `MermaidPipeline::export()` becomes `NativeRenderer::image()`.
- Conversion goes through contracts: each language has a `Source\Reader` and a `Source\Writer` (`MermaidReader`, `DotReader`, `MermaidWriter`, `DotWriter`, `PlantUmlWriter`, now instances), gathered in `Source\Languages`. Models implement `Model\Diagram` and name their `Model\Family`; `Converter::write()` is an instance method that sends a family directly or through its graph, as [docs/conversion.md](docs/conversion.md) lists. The former static readers and writers become `Mermaid\Source\MermaidGraphReader`, `MermaidGraphWriter`, `GraphViz\Source\DotGraphReader`, `DotGraphWriter` and `PlantUML\Source\PlantUmlGraphWriter`.
- A source that does not parse is refused as `The <language> source cannot be read: …`; parsers' errors implement `Source\ParseError`.
- One `Process\CommandRenderer` replaces `MmdcRenderer`, `DotRenderer::render()` and `PlantUmlRenderer`; `MermaidCommand`, `DotCommand` and `PlantUmlCommand` implement `Process\Command`. `GraphViz\DotRenderer` becomes `GraphViz\DotTool`, which keeps `layout()` and `json()`. A temporary mmdc directory is now named `viz-mmdc-…`.
- `Viz` takes an `engine` (`Render\Engine::Auto`, `Native` or `Cli`) and draws every language through a `FallbackRenderer`, native first where a native renderer exists.
- Graph layout has a neutral seam: `Layout\Engine\LayoutEngine` places a `LayoutGraph` of measured nodes, clusters and edges into a `Placement`. Mermaid's ranked layout becomes `Layout\Ranked\RankedEngine` (from `CompoundGraphPlacement`), with `Layout\Ranked\RankedBoxes` and `Ranks` (from `MermaidRanks`); `Mermaid\MermaidLayoutInput` measures a Mermaid graph for it, and `RankedGraphLayout` takes any engine. `MermaidPlacement` gives way to `Placement`, whose `routes` were `lanes` and whose directions are `Model\Direction`.
- `GraphViz\DotColor` becomes `Color\ColorValue`; `Mermaid\CssColorNames` becomes `Color\CssColorNames`.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-19

First release, extracted from the Porta IDE's graph viewer.

- Native Mermaid 12.1: parsing, layout, themes and SVG/PNG drawing for 30+ diagram families, now with a native vector PDF backend.
- mermaid-cli, Graphviz and PlantUML renderers over their command-line tools, synchronous, with configurable paths.
- `FallbackRenderer`, `Exporter` and the `Viz` facade.
- A shared, layout-free diagram model with Mermaid, DOT and PlantUML writers, and Mermaid flowchart and DOT readers.
